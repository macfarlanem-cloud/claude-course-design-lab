# Who is in AGRI 110 this term

Sample learner profile for the lab. Invented, and deliberately described by role, place, and circumstance rather than by labels. Replace it with a description of your own learners.

## The section

- 28 learners enrolled, fully online and asynchronous.
- Ages span from 17 (a dual-enrolled high school student) to a learner in their sixties who is planning a second career on a small acreage.
- About a third work full time and log in mostly on evenings and weekends. Several are parents.
- Roughly half are first in their family to attend college.
- A handful already work in agriculture: two on family orchards, one at a feed store, one at a county agricultural office. Most have never worked on a farm and enrolled out of curiosity about food, climate, or a possible career change.
- Several learners live in rural areas where home internet is unreliable; they often do coursework on a phone.
- Reading load is a common source of stress. Learners have said in past terms that long textbook chapters plus long recorded lectures in the same week feel like too much.

## What they bring

- Lived experience with food, land, water, and work that is rarely invited into the course.
- Strong practical problem-solving from jobs, families, and communities.
- Real questions about cost, time, and whether "sustainable" is affordable for a working farm.

## What the instructor has noticed

- Participation drops in Weeks 6 and 7, right after the first quiz and before the midterm.
- Learners engage most when a task connects to a place or a decision they recognize.
- Learners appreciate knowing exactly what "good" looks like before they submit.
